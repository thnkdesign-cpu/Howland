What each package includes

Each package builds on the one before it — shared foundation, plus what sets it apart (in bold)

The Essentials

The foundation — a redesigned homepage and the fixes that carry across the whole site.

$10,800

Included

  • Discovery — Divi feasibility audit only
  • Full homepage redesign
  • Site-wide navigation overhaul — hamburger removed, full desktop nav, every page surfaced, persistent CTA, redesigned footer
  • New brand & visual system — typography, color, and WCAG accessibility, applied site-wide
  • Site speed & mobile display fixed site-wide
  • Contact form restyled to the new look
  • Supporting pages carry the new look — video headers get a fallback image; no additional pages individually designed

Moves to a later stage

  • Individually designed hero + CTA on About & Portfolios
  • Full content redesign of the About & Portfolios bodies
  • A fully rebuilt, native contact form
  • A refreshed, shareable blog
  • Analytics, SEO, GEO, advertising & conversion tracking
  • Closing-pitch CTA links on About & every blog post
