The four directions in detail

Comprehensive

The complete plan — every page designed and built, homepage sets the system the rest inherits.

$10,450–$13,950
fully loaded · $450–$3,950 over

Included

  • Divi feasibility audit + conversion & design brief
  • Full homepage redesign + shared system (nav, footer, type, CTAs)
  • About & Portfolios redesigned and rebuilt
  • Full blog reading / sharing rebuild
  • On-brand contact form + site-wide video removal

Not included

  • Fee schedule / FAQ redesign
  • Conversion tracking (deferred — we'd add it back before launch)
  • Analytics-platform cleanup (separate change order)
  • SEO & ad tuning (a later phase)

Ramifications

Delivers the most against Rob's original brief — but lands over $10k once PM is counted, by $450 at best and up to ~$3,950 if the audit surfaces surprises. Choosing this means either a scope-for-price conversation or accepting a higher number.

Delivery $9,450–$12,450+PM $1,000–$1,500=$10,450–$13,950

Lean Core

Spend where traffic and value are — homepage + blog + shared system; other pages stabilized, not polished.

$9,550–$12,750
fully loaded · $450 under → $2,750 over

Included

  • Divi audit + conversion & design brief
  • Full homepage + shared system
  • Full blog rebuild, with a booking CTA built into the post template
  • Site-wide video removal + static fallback images on About/Portfolios
  • Contact-form reskin to the new look

Not included

  • Full redesign of About / Portfolios (bodies left as-is)
  • Page-by-page closing CTAs beyond the blog
  • Fee / FAQ redesign
  • Conversion tracking

Ramifications

Fits at the low end, over at the high end. Keeps the blog — a commitment from the 9/8 meeting — and fixes the tech everywhere. The trade: About & Portfolios keep their current body layout under a cleaner header, so they'll read as lighter-touch next to the homepage.

Delivery $8,550–$11,250+PM $1,000–$1,500=$9,550–$12,750

Homepage-Only

Make one page excellent, fix the tech across the whole site, establish the system — then expand.

$7,900–$10,800
fully loaded · $2,100 under → $800 over

Included

  • Divi audit + conversion & design brief
  • Full homepage + shared system (appears site-wide)
  • Site-wide video removal + fallback images
  • Contact-form reskin

Not included

  • Blog rebuild (deferred to a next stage)
  • About / Portfolios redesign (video fix only)
  • Page-level closing CTAs
  • Fee / FAQ; conversion tracking

Ramifications

The only direction that fits $10k with real margin. But it's a front-door facelift, not a full redesign — a prospect who clicks past the homepage still meets the old site — and it sets aside the blog goal from the 9/8 meeting.

Best positioned to Rob as stage one of a staged rollout: the design system makes the blog and other pages cheaper fast-follows, and the ~$2k of low-end margin could fund the blog back first.

Delivery $6,900–$9,300+PM $1,000–$1,500=$7,900–$10,800

Design-Only

Design the entire site to the full vision; hand implementation to WebSalt or a separate build project.

$8,050–$10,500
fully loaded · $1,950 under → $500 over

Included

  • Discovery + Divi feasibility check
  • Full design for every page — home, about, portfolios, blog, contact, fee/FAQ
  • Design→build handoff package: annotated comps, style tokens, assets

Not included

  • All implementation (→ WebSalt or a secondary project)
  • Nothing goes live this phase
  • The tech problems aren't fixed until someone builds

Ramifications

Delivers the complete design vision within budget, and it's the most cost-certain option — no build unknowns on our side. But nothing improves on the live site in Phase 1, and it routes the build to WebSalt, the vendor we deliberately planned to work around (or implies a ~$15k two-project total if built in-house later).

Strong as a definition step: once the whole site is designed, the build can be quoted precisely.

Delivery $7,050–$9,000+PM $1,000–$1,500=$8,050–$10,500

How to read the ranges. All figures are fully loaded (delivery @ $150/hr + Amber's PM). Ranges are wide where a short Phase 1 Divi audit hasn't yet resolved two unknowns: whether the contact form is a locked third-party embed, and how cluttered the About/Portfolios page bodies are. The audit narrows both before any number is locked.

What every option shares. The hero/header videos come off site-wide — that's the root fix for the load and mobile-viewport problems — and the homepage always establishes the type, contrast, and layout system the rest of the site inherits.

The real fork. Homepage-Only and Design-Only both fit the budget from opposite directions — build one page well, or design everything and build later. Comprehensive and Lean Core deliver more of the site but lean on the top of the budget or past it.
